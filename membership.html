Membership details, service terms and exclusions

Superior 365 is a year round roof protection, preventive maintenance, monitoring, and documentation membership designed to help property owners identify roof concerns early, maintain important roof components, document roof condition over time, and receive priority service when roof related needs arise.

Superior 365 is not an insurance policy, home warranty, or guarantee against all roof leaks, storm damage, deterioration, or future repairs. Membership benefits are limited to the services specifically included in the selected membership level and are subject to the eligibility requirements, exclusions, service limitations, and other terms described below.

By enrolling in Superior 365, the Member acknowledges and agrees to these Membership Details, Service Terms & Exclusions.

1. Membership Levels

Bronze: Roof Monitoring

$19.95 a month or $199 a year

Bronze is designed for property owners who want professional roof monitoring, documentation, and priority access to Superior Roofing Specialists.

Included Benefits

  • Digital Photo Report
  • Roof Health Score
  • Permanent Superior 365 Roof Record
  • Storm Watch and severe weather alerts
  • Insurance and warranty documentation assistance
  • 48 hour priority scheduling
  • 5% Member Savings on qualifying roof repairs

Bronze Service Limitations

Bronze does not include routine preventive maintenance, gutter cleaning, emergency tarping, leak repairs, or the Superior 365 Leak Protection Guarantee.

Any repair, maintenance, replacement, or other work identified during an inspection will be quoted separately unless specifically stated otherwise in writing.

2. Silver: Preventive Roof Care

$29.95 a month or $299 a year

Most popular

Silver includes everything provided with Bronze plus additional preventive roof care services.

Included Benefits

Included preventive maintenance is intended for minor maintenance of existing, serviceable roof components.

Included maintenance does not constitute major roof repair or replacement.

If Superior Roofing Specialists determines that a component is damaged, deteriorated, improperly installed, unsafe, beyond normal maintenance, or requires replacement rather than routine maintenance, the Member will be informed and the necessary work may be quoted separately.

4. Roof Health Inspections

A Superior 365 Roof Health Inspection is a visual and, when appropriate, technology assisted evaluation of reasonably accessible roof areas and related components.

Depending on the property and membership level, an inspection may include evaluation of:

  • Roofing materials
  • Flashings
  • Roof penetrations
  • Pipe boots
  • Vents
  • Skylights
  • Sheet metal components
  • Gutters and drainage conditions
  • Visible sealant conditions
  • Visible storm related concerns
  • Attic moisture or ventilation conditions when included and reasonably accessible

An inspection is intended to identify visible conditions present at the time of inspection.

An inspection is not an engineering evaluation, building code certification, mold inspection, environmental assessment, structural certification, or guarantee that hidden defects or future failures do not exist.

Superior Roofing Specialists is not responsible for conditions that could not reasonably be observed during the inspection.

5. Digital Photo Report & Roof Health Score

When included in the selected membership, Superior Roofing Specialists will maintain documentation of the roof’s observable condition.

Reports may contain photographs, observations, recommendations, maintenance history, and a Roof Health Score.

The Roof Health Score is an internal condition assessment tool intended to help Members understand the general observed condition of their roofing system.

It is not a guarantee of remaining roof life, insurability, code compliance, manufacturer warranty eligibility, or future roof performance.

6. Permanent Superior 365 Roof Record

Superior Roofing Specialists may maintain a digital history of inspections, photographs, maintenance activities, repairs, storm inspections, and other roof related services performed or documented through Superior 365.

This record can provide useful documentation when discussing:

  • Insurance claims
  • Manufacturer warranties
  • Roof repairs
  • Property maintenance
  • Storm history
  • Future roof replacement
  • Property sale or transfer

Superior Roofing Specialists does not guarantee that an insurance carrier, manufacturer, buyer, lender, adjuster, or other third party will accept Superior 365 documentation for any particular purpose.

7. Storm Watch & Severe Weather Alerts

Superior 365 may provide Members with notifications regarding significant weather or storm activity that may affect the Member’s property.

Weather information may be obtained from third party data providers and is provided as a convenience.

Superior Roofing Specialists does not guarantee that every weather event will be identified, that every Member will receive an alert before or immediately following an event, or that an alert confirms the Member’s property sustained damage.

Members remain responsible for contacting Superior Roofing Specialists if they suspect roof damage.

8. Post Storm Inspections

When included in the selected membership, Members may receive priority access to post storm inspections.

Priority status means Superior 365 Members will generally be scheduled ahead of comparable non member service requests when operationally practical.

Priority service does not guarantee immediate, same day, or next day service.

Following widespread severe weather, tornadoes, hailstorms, high winds, natural disasters, or other events creating unusually high demand, service times may be extended.

All services remain subject to weather conditions, employee safety, road accessibility, property accessibility, equipment availability, and overall service demand.

9. 48 Hour Priority Scheduling

Where 48 hour priority scheduling is included, Superior Roofing Specialists will use commercially reasonable efforts to prioritize the Member’s service request.

The 48 hour period is a scheduling priority target and does not constitute a guarantee that inspection, repair, or other work will be completed within 48 hours.

Severe weather, emergencies, weekends, holidays, unsafe working conditions, material availability, unusually high demand, or circumstances outside Superior Roofing Specialists’ reasonable control may affect scheduling.

10. Emergency Roof Response: Gold

Gold Members receive priority emergency roof response status.

Emergency roof response is intended to help evaluate and, where reasonably possible, temporarily mitigate active roof related water intrusion or significant roof openings.

Emergency response does not mean permanent repair is included.

Any permanent repair or roof replacement required following emergency service will be evaluated and, unless otherwise covered by a specific written Superior 365 benefit, quoted separately.

11. Emergency Tarping Benefit: Gold

The Emergency Tarping Benefit is intended to provide reasonable temporary protection following a qualifying roof emergency.

Emergency tarping is a temporary mitigation measure and is not a permanent roof repair.

Tarping may be provided only when Superior Roofing Specialists determines that:

  • Temporary tarping is reasonably necessary;
  • The affected area can be safely accessed;
  • Weather conditions permit safe installation;
  • Tarping will not create an unreasonable risk of additional property damage; and
  • The condition falls within the scope of the Superior 365 program.

The benefit applies to reasonable temporary mitigation only.

Specialty equipment, extensive temporary structures, structural stabilization, tree removal, electrical hazards, hazardous material conditions, unusually large damaged areas, or other extraordinary circumstances may require additional charges or third party services.

Superior Roofing Specialists may decline or postpone tarping when conditions are unsafe.

Emergency tarping does not include permanent repair of the underlying roof damage.

12. Superior 365 Leak Protection Guarantee: Gold

The Superior 365 Leak Protection Guarantee is available only to qualifying Gold Members and qualifying roofing systems.

The purpose of the Guarantee is to provide eligible Gold Members with additional protection against qualifying roof leaks during an active membership.

Eligibility

To qualify, the roof must:

  • Be inspected and accepted into the Leak Protection Guarantee by Superior Roofing Specialists;
  • Be maintained as reasonably required by Superior Roofing Specialists;
  • Have all required or recommended eligibility repairs completed;
  • Remain continuously enrolled in an active Gold membership; and
  • Remain reasonably accessible and safe to inspect and service.

Superior Roofing Specialists may require corrective work before activating or continuing Leak Protection Guarantee eligibility.

What the Guarantee Covers

If a qualifying leak occurs in an eligible roofing area during an active Gold membership, Superior Roofing Specialists will evaluate the source and determine whether the condition qualifies under the Guarantee.

For a covered condition, Superior Roofing Specialists’ obligation is limited to the roof related corrective action specifically authorized under the Guarantee.

What the Guarantee Does Not Cover

Unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing, the Leak Protection Guarantee does not cover:

  • Storm, hail, tornado, hurricane, wind, lightning, flood, earthquake, fire, or other casualty damage
  • Damage caused by falling trees, branches, debris, animals, pests, or foreign objects
  • Structural movement or building settlement
  • Foundation movement
  • Condensation
  • Plumbing leaks
  • HVAC related moisture
  • Interior humidity or moisture conditions
  • Mold or microbial growth
  • Damage originating from walls, windows, doors, siding, masonry, chimneys, or other non roof components
  • Damage caused by solar panels, satellite dishes, antennas, mechanical equipment, or third party roof penetrations
  • Damage caused by another contractor or person accessing or modifying the roof
  • Vandalism, abuse, neglect, or intentional damage
  • Pre existing leaks or defects
  • Conditions specifically identified as requiring repair that the Member declined or failed to correct
  • Roofing systems or portions of a roof that were not accepted into the Guarantee
  • Interior property damage or personal property losses

The Leak Protection Guarantee is a limited membership benefit. It is not an insurance policy and does not replace homeowners, commercial property, or other insurance coverage.

13. Pre Existing Conditions

Superior 365 does not automatically cover defects, damage, deterioration, leaks, installation deficiencies, code issues, or other conditions existing before enrollment.

If an initial inspection identifies conditions requiring corrective work, Superior Roofing Specialists may:

  • Exclude the affected area from certain membership benefits; or
  • Require repairs before activating specific membership benefits.

Payment of a membership fee does not obligate Superior Roofing Specialists to repair a pre existing condition without additional charge.

Membership does not guarantee that thermal imaging or drone equipment will be used during every inspection.

Important summary

Superior 365 is designed around four core principles:

  • Monitor. Regular professional roof evaluations help identify concerns before they become larger problems.
  • Maintain. Silver and Gold memberships provide preventive roof care services designed to address qualifying maintenance items.
  • Document. Superior 365 creates an ongoing roof record with inspections, photographs, condition information, and service history.
  • Respond. Members receive priority access to Superior Roofing Specialists when roof concerns and severe weather events occur.

Superior 365 does not promise that a roof will never leak, eliminate the need for future repairs or replacement, or replace property insurance.

Instead, the program provides an ongoing professional relationship focused on monitoring, preventive care, documentation, and priority response.
