Common questions

I can't sign in

Sign in with the email address on your membership. If you have forgotten your password, tap Forgot password? on the sign in screen. We will email you a code, and you can choose a new password in the app. Still stuck? Call or email us.

I signed up but don't see my plan

Your plan appears once your membership is active and the email you signed up with matches the one on your membership. Memberships are set up by our office. Call us and we will link it for you.

How do I join or change my plan?

Memberships are sold and billed by Superior Roofing Specialists, not through the App Store. Visit srsokc.com/memberships or call us. See the membership page for what each plan includes.

How do I request service?

Open the menu and choose Request service. Pick what you need, say how urgent it is, add a note and a photo if it helps, and send. You can follow it under Your requests.

Where are my inspection photos?

Open the menu and choose Your roof. Each inspection has its photos, our notes on each area, and a report you can save or share.

How do storm alerts work?

When hail or high wind is reported near the property on your membership, the app lets you know and you can book a storm inspection. You can turn Storm Watch on or off on the Home screen, and manage notifications in your iPhone Settings under Notifications, then Superior 365.

How do I delete my account?

Open the menu and choose Delete account. You can also email info@srsokc.com and we will do it for you. The Privacy Policy explains what is removed and what is kept as your property's service record.
